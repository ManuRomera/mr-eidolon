# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-28)
- First release. Complete adventure in **English and Spanish** (two Adventure compendiums; auto-import in an empty world in Foundry’s language).
- Warden’s Dossier as journals, Warden’s Screen checked against the Player’s Survival Guide 1.2, editable Absence Log.
- Deck A and Deck B with walls, doors, room notes and stair/hatch teleports between decks; labeled crew and Warden plans.
- Nine image scenes and two deck scenes registered in MR- Dirección (read-aloud, music, motifs, effects, cast, handouts, clocks and evidence milestones).
- Absence Log tool: Imprint per person, copies, impartial replacement draw.
- 6 pregens, 8 survivors, 2 threats with round tokens; 10 handouts; 3 roll tables; 2 macros; 10 tracks and 15 effects.
- PDFs (A4 dossier, pregens and handouts, A3 deck plans) in both languages attached to the release.
